# Eli Dream World

A 3D character / farm / build game — play in the browser on laptop or phone.

## Play online

**https://6tbwmzr522-crypto.github.io/dream-world/**

Repo: https://github.com/6tbwmzr522-crypto/dream-world

## Phone controls

- **Left joystick** — move
- **Drag on the world** — look around
- **Use** — interact (leash, saddle, ride). Also a helper to pop in or out of a door.
- **Jump / Run** — as labeled
- **Build / Inventory / Menu** — top-right buttons
- Walk straight through a house door to go inside. Walk back out to leave. Stairs go to a second floor.
- In build mode: tap to place, **Remove** to delete. Tap a table/chair/sofa/bed then tap a new spot, or drag it.

## Laptop controls

WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · Shift run · Walk in a door (E helps) · B build · Drag table/chair/sofa/bed to move · I inventory · Right-click remove
